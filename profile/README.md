# LDataLab

**Data Science · IA · Public Policy**

LDataLab is a space for teaching, learning, and experimenting with **data science and IA**, with a particular interest in their application to **public policy, economics, and social problems**.

The organization brings together course materials, examples, computational tools, and projects designed to make data analysis **reproducible, transparent, and useful for real-world decision making**.

## What you'll find here

Repositories in this organization may include:

* 📚 **Course materials** on programming, data science, and IA.
* 🐍 **Python notebooks and examples** for learning data analysis.
* 📊 **Data visualization and exploratory analysis**.
* 🔬 **Applied data science projects**.
* 🧩 Examples of **reproducible computational workflows** using tools such as Git, Python, Jupyter, Quarto, and related technologies.
* 🏛️ Applications of data and computational methods to **public policy and social science**.

## Teaching philosophy

The goal is not only to learn how to use computational tools, but also to understand **when, why, and how to use them**.

Materials emphasize:

* problem formulation before modeling;
* clear and reproducible workflows;
* understanding data before applying algorithms;
* interpretation alongside computation;
* responsible use of data and artificial intelligence.

## Courses

Some repositories correspond to courses and workshops in areas such as:

* Introduction to Programming
* Data Science
* Computational Methods
* Data Science for Public Policy
* Data Science for Economics
* GovTech and Digital Transformation

Each course repository contains its own syllabus, materials, exercises, and instructions.
